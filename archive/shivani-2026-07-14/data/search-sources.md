# Platform-First Job Search Sources

Use job platforms and search engines first. Prioritize medical coordinator, patient access, patient services, referral, intake, and medical office coordination roles. Home care is optional/adjacent and should not drive the search unless explicitly requested.

## Primary Discovery Order

1. Google Jobs
2. LinkedIn Jobs
3. Glassdoor
4. ZipRecruiter
5. CareerBuilder / Talent.com / Lensa / JobLeads as secondary aggregators
6. Employer career page only after the posting looks worth applying to
